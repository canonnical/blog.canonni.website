My old DnD group (shoutout to Rodin if you’re reading this btw) grew increasingly tired with our dull Homebrew campaign after yet another fort one day and decided to do what 🤓s do best: A Meta Game™. Instead of roleplaying as oversexualized mythical characters, we opted to create original personas based on ourselves, with each member working on their impression of the next one clockwise, and so forth. I unfortunately succumbed to the lowly fate of being characterized by a brute I had beef with, who assigned and justified the following ability scores to “Omar the Orangutan”:

- 7 for Strength, owing that I “have no muscles, and I mean like, zero haha”
- 11 for Dexterity, since “bro can jump and shit”
- 9 for Constitution, with no reason given
- 16 for Intelligence, due to “nerddddd” while pulling out the pen in my shirt pocket
- 17 for Wisdom, as a result of “you know like literally everything. What’s that word you use? ‘Eccentric?’” *ehh… it’s eclectic but close enough.*
- 12 Charisma, as “you’re a 4 at best but the way you talk is lowkey cool”

…thanks bro. Thanks a lot. Like most men, none of these stats feel large enough to my primitave mind, but if there’s one I resoundingly concur with and would even argue for it to be lowered, it’s dexterity and athletics in particular.
